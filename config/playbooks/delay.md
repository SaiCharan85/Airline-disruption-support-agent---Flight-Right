# Playbook: delay

1. **Urgent care first.** If the passenger is at the airport and care is owed (see the entitlement),
   say what they get now (refreshments, meals, calls, hotel if overnight) and how to get it (vouchers
   issued, or keep receipts for reimbursement).
2. **Current status.** Give the latest estimated departure from flight status; never guess a time.
3. **Options.** If the departure delay reaches 5 hours, offer the refund option alongside continuing
   the journey. Offer rebooking only where the entitlement allows it.
4. **Compensation.** State the amount only if the entitlement gives one and its confidence is
   verified; it depends on the arrival delay at the final destination, so for an ongoing delay say
   it will be assessed on arrival. If the airline cites extraordinary circumstances, say so plainly
   and confirm care still applies.
5. **Vouchers.** Never offer a travel voucher instead of cash without stating the cash right.
6. **Next step and timeline.** Who does what next, and when the passenger will hear back.
