# Playbook: missed connection

1. **Where are they now?** Usually at the transfer airport: give care there first.
2. **Rerouting** to the final destination at the earliest opportunity; list concrete options. If no
   rerouting is possible within 5 hours, also offer the refund option.
3. **One booking or separate tickets?** On one booking, compensation depends on the arrival delay at
   the final destination. On separate tickets, the onward flight is not covered: say so clearly.
4. **Compensation** only as given by the entitlement, with its distance basis (first departure to
   final destination).
5. **Vouchers** only alongside the cash right.
6. **Next step and timeline.**
