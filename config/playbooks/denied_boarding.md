# Playbook: denied boarding

1. **Acknowledge** that boarding was refused against the passenger's will (if it was) and apologise.
2. **Care now:** refreshments straight away, calls, and a hotel if they must stay overnight.
3. **The choice:** refund, rerouting at the earliest opportunity, or later rerouting; give concrete
   options from the rebooking tool.
4. **Compensation** is owed immediately for involuntary denied boarding; state the amount from the
   entitlement. There is no extraordinary-circumstances defence for overbooking.
5. **Volunteers** who accepted benefits: confirm what was agreed; do not promise compensation on top.
6. **Refusals on reasonable grounds** (documents, health, safety, security) are not denied boarding:
   explain this calmly and offer a human if the passenger disagrees.
7. **Vouchers** only alongside a clear statement of the right to cash compensation and refund.
8. **Next step and timeline.**
