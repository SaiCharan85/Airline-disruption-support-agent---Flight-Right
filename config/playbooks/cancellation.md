# Playbook: cancellation

1. **Urgent care first.** For a same-day cancellation, give care straight away (refreshments,
   meals, calls; hotel and transfer if the new flight is the next day).
2. **The choice.** The passenger chooses between a full refund (paid within the deadline in the
   entitlement), rerouting at the earliest opportunity, or rerouting at a later date of their choice.
   List concrete rebooking options from the rebooking tool, earliest arrival first, including other
   airlines, alternative airports or rail where offered.
3. **Compensation.** State it only as given by the entitlement (notice period and rerouting window
   decide it). Mention a reduction only if the entitlement lists one, and then only as something the
   airline may apply.
4. **Extraordinary circumstances.** If cited, name the reason; care and refund/rerouting rights remain.
5. **Vouchers.** Only alongside a clear statement of the cash refund right.
